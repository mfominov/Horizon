# Evaluation goal

Score how attractive this item is for a personal home lab, self-hosting, learning, or automation experiment.

# Weighted criteria

- Technical novelty and personal learning value: 30%.
- Self-hosting capability: 25%.
- Ease of launch and experimentation: 20%.
- Automation potential: 15%.
- Operating cost: 10%.

# Scoring guidance

Return a score from 0 to 10. Apply the weights consistently. A 9-10 item is compelling and practical to try; 7-8 deserves a near-term experiment; 5-6 is interesting but conditional; 3-4 has high friction or weak personal value; 0-2 is noise or clearly unsuitable.

Do not assume self-hosting, low cost, or easy setup when the source does not show it. State decisive evidence and unknowns in the reason. Use three to five specific tags.
